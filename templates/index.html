Farmer Measurement Guide

Use measured values from your farm or a trusted local source. Do not guess values just to obtain a recommendation.

1. Nitrogen (N), Phosphorus (P) and Potassium (K)

These are soil nutrient measurements. Collect a representative soil sample and submit it to a soil-testing laboratory, KVK, or an authorised soil testing centre. Enter the N, P and K values exactly as reported, using the same units expected by this model.

2. Temperature (°C)

Use a thermometer or a nearby reliable weather station. Enter the temperature in degrees Celsius. Use a value representative of the crop's growing conditions.

3. Humidity (%)

Use a digital hygrometer or a reliable weather station. Enter relative humidity as a percentage from 0 to 100.

4. Soil pH

Use a calibrated soil pH meter according to its instructions, or obtain the value from a soil-testing laboratory. Soil pH indicates acidity or alkalinity.

5. Rainfall (mm)

Use a rain gauge or a reliable local weather station. Enter rainfall in millimetres for the same measurement period used by the model's training data.

Important: The model expects the same units, sampling method and measurement period used in its training dataset. A soil report may use different nutrient units or extraction methods. Ask the laboratory or an agricultural officer to interpret the report before entering values.

Where can I get help?

  • Visit your nearest Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK).
  • Contact an authorised soil-testing laboratory.
  • Ask your local agriculture department about soil testing and Soil Health Card services.
